## 5. Base Ultramatter: UMP and UMN

### 5.1 Nuclear Integration

UMP and UMN are analogs of protons and neutrons. They may occupy positions within ordinary atomic nuclei.

- They are **not** antimatter
- They do **not** annihilate with normal matter
- A nucleus containing UM particles behaves externally as a stable isotope until activation

---

## 6. Base UM Activation

Base UM requires compatible UM particles. When activated:

1. UM begins intercepting local UE
2. Intercepted UE is converted to kinetic energy
3. An ultra-vacuum region forms as UE is depleted to zero
4. Matter inside is displaced using the converted kinetic energy

**Activation can occur through:**
- Electromagnetic stimulation
- Mechanical energy
- Thermal excitation
- Particle interaction

---

## 7. The Ultra-Vacuum

An ultra-vacuum is a temporary localized region in which UE has been fully intercepted, leaving quantum mechanics with no substrate to run on.

**Characterized by:**
- Complete local UE depletion
- Absence of ordinary matter (displaced at activation)
- Full collapse of quantum mechanical behavior
- Classical propagation of any radiation that enters

The ultra-vacuum is not merely empty space. Space and geometry continue to exist normally. The **UE supply has been cut**, and with it every physical process that depends on quantum mechanics.

---

## 8. Quantum Suspension

Inside an ultra-vacuum, quantum behavior collapses entirely because UE is absent. This is not suppression by force — it is the natural consequence of removing the substrate QM requires.

### 8.1 Zero-Point Energy
Local quantum fields no longer possess ground-state energy. UE that sustained them has been intercepted.

### 8.2 Virtual Particles
Virtual particle production ceases. No UE means no quantum fluctuation budget.

### 8.3 Casimir Effects
Vacuum-mediated forces disappear.

### 8.4 Electron Orbitals
Electrons no longer occupy quantum orbitals. They follow **classical trajectories** until UE is restored.

### 8.5 Quantum Tunneling
Suppressed entirely. Tunneling probability depends on quantum field structure that UE normally maintains.

### 8.6 Uncertainty
Particles temporarily possess definite classical positions and momenta.

### 8.7 Spin
Quantum spin behaves as classical angular momentum.

### 8.8 Quantum Coherence
All quantum coherence collapses. Superpositions resolve to classical states instantly.

---

## 9. Ultra-Vacuum Formation and Matter Displacement

The ultra-vacuum forms instantaneously at its activation location. Once formed, it remains **fixed in space** — moving the originating UM particle after activation does not move the ultra-vacuum.

### 9.1 Displacement Mechanism

Immediately upon formation, UM intercepts the UE of every atom in the affected volume and converts it to outward kinetic energy. Every atom whose position lies within the newly created ultra-vacuum is instantaneously assigned an **outward velocity** toward the nearest point outside the boundary:

$$v_{atom} = \sqrt{\frac{2 E_{UE,atom}}{m_{atom}}}$$

Because UE density is uniform at baseline, UE available per unit volume is constant. However, the UE-to-mass ratio varies by element — lighter atoms receive higher exit velocities than heavier atoms from the same interception volume. **Different materials are displaced at different velocities** from the same UM event.

### 9.2 Consequences of Displacement

- Extreme compression at the vacuum boundary
- Shock waves in surrounding material
- Plasma formation
- Temporary nuclear fusion conditions
- Intense thermal release

---

## 10. Radiation Inside an Ultra-Vacuum

Radiation entering an ultra-vacuum encounters a UE-depleted environment that cannot support bound UE particles.

**Upon crossing the boundary:**
1. The radiation's bound UE particle is stripped and absorbed into the vacuum's UE budget, incrementally strengthening the vacuum
2. The radiation propagates **classically** inside — no interference, no polarization quantization, no decay
3. Energy, momentum, and direction are preserved exactly

**Upon re-quantization:**
- The radiation reacquires a bound UE particle as UE floods back in
- Quantum behavior resumes immediately
- The radiation exits unchanged in energy, momentum, and direction

> **Exploitable property:** An ultra-vacuum bombarded with radiation during its lifetime is continuously fed additional UE from incoming bound particles. Sustained radiation exposure actively extends and strengthens the vacuum. This is not a side effect — it is an engineerable mechanic.

---

## 11. Collapse, Re-Quantization, and UE Restoration

When the ultra-vacuum expires, UE self-balancing pressure floods UE back into the depleted region from the surrounding field.

**Re-quantization sequence:**
- UE refills the depleted volume from the boundary inward
- Quantum fields reform progressively as UE reaches each region
- Zero-point energy is restored
- Electron orbitals reform
- Vacuum fluctuations resume
- Radiation inside reacquires bound UE particles
- Quantum coherence is restored

The inward rush of UE refilling the void produces the **re-quantization convergence pulse** (see [Section 35](06-mathematics.md#35-re-quantization-pulse)).

---

## 12. UE Depletion and Cooldown

After an ultra-vacuum collapses, the affected region is not immediately available for further UM activation. UE must replenish to baseline $\rho_{UE}$ before QM can fully resume and before another UM event can draw from that region effectively.

**The cooldown period:**

$$t_{cooldown} = \frac{r}{c}$$

where $r$ is the radius of the collapsed ultra-vacuum and $c$ is the speed of light — the time for UE to flood back from the boundary to the center.

**Consequences:**
- Large ultra-vacuums have proportionally longer cooldowns
- A second UM activation in the same region during cooldown draws from reduced UE and produces a proportionally weaker effect
- Overlapping activations can be timed to exploit partial UE depletion deliberately

**Note:** UE tunneling via entanglement can partially bypass cooldown by feeding UE into a depleted region from a remote partner (see [Section 22](04-entanglement.md#22-ue-tunneling)).
