## 15. UMxH Particles

UMxH particles are **self-activating** ultramatter particles. Upon activation they:

1. Intercept local UE and generate an ultra-vacuum
2. Emit an omnidirectional activation propagation
3. Select a compatible UMxL particle

The activation propagation is an **isotropic quantum interaction**, not a physical wave. The compatible UMxL **nearest by distance** is selected. Only one UMxL is selected per activation event.

> **Targeting implication:** Shielding a UMxL with closer UMxL particles redirects the H signal to the nearer particle.

---

## 16. UMxL Particles and Radiation-Bound UE

UMxL particles are **passive radiation and bound UE accumulators**. They absorb:
- Electromagnetic radiation
- Particle radiation
- Cosmic radiation
- Nuclear radiation

For each absorbed radiation quantum, UMxL captures both the radiation and its **bound UE particle**. The storage domain accumulates:
- A radiation archive (energy, momentum, direction, polarization, state)
- A UE reservoir built from accumulated bound UE particles

**UMxL cannot store free UE** — only UE that arrives bound to radiation.

---

## 17. Radiation Storage Domain

The UMxL storage domain preserves radiation with perfect fidelity.

**Stored radiation retains:**
- Energy
- Momentum
- Direction
- Polarization
- State information

**Stored radiation does not:**
- Decay
- Lose energy
- Interact
- Age

**Why fidelity is perfect:** Inside the storage domain, radiation is separated from the UE flow that normally governs quantum behavior. No UE means no QM means no decay, no redshift, no state evolution. Storage fidelity is the automatic consequence of UE isolation, not a special property of UMxL.

---

## 18. UMxL Activation

UMxL cannot activate independently under standard conditions.

**Valid activation pathways:**
1. **H-signal activation** — A compatible UMxH selects this UMxL via isotropic propagation
2. **UME local detonation** — A UME physically co-located in the same atom triggers UMxL directly
3. **Entanglement relay** — Via quantum connection to an activated UMxH or UME

**Upon activation:**
1. Stored radiation begins release
2. Accumulated bound UE particles are injected into the local UE field
3. The UE injection expands the available interception budget, increasing ultra-vacuum radius beyond baseline
4. An ultra-vacuum is generated drawing from both local UE and the injected UE reservoir
